## Research Question

What are the primary benefits and risks of Large Language Models (LLMs)?

---

## Summary

Large Language Models (LLMs) have become valuable tools for generating text, answering questions, assisting with programming, and improving productivity across many industries.

Their major benefits include faster information processing, automation of repetitive tasks, multilingual communication, and support for education and business workflows.

However, LLMs also present challenges such as hallucinations, bias, misinformation, privacy concerns, and excessive reliance on AI-generated content.

Responsible development requires continuous evaluation, human oversight, and transparent safety practices.

---

## Key Findings

### Benefits

- Improved productivity
- Faster content generation
- Language translation
- Coding assistance
- Educational support

### Risks

- Hallucinated information
- Dataset bias
- Privacy concerns
- Overconfidence
- Ethical misuse

---

## Conclusion

Large Language Models offer significant opportunities but require careful evaluation and responsible use to ensure reliable and trustworthy outputs.
