# Annotation Guidelines

## Purpose

This document outlines the evaluation criteria I use when assessing AI-generated responses.

---

## Evaluation Criteria

### 1. Accuracy

Is the information factually correct?

### 2. Instruction Following

Did the response follow every instruction in the prompt?

### 3. Completeness

Did it answer every part of the user's request?

### 4. Clarity

Is the response easy to understand?

### 5. Safety

Does the response avoid harmful, misleading, or unsafe advice?

### 6. Tone

Is the tone appropriate for the context?

---

## Rating Scale

| Score | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| 5 | Excellent |
| 4 | Good |
| 3 | Acceptable |
| 2 | Needs Improvement |
| 1 | Poor |
