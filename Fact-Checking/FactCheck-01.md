# Fact-Checking #1

**Category:** Fact Verification  
**Skills Demonstrated:** Research, Critical Thinking, Accuracy Verification

---

## Claim

> "The Great Wall of China is visible from space with the naked eye."

---

## Verification Process

### Source Review

The claim has been repeated for many years but has been disproven by astronauts and space agencies.

According to NASA, the Great Wall is generally **not visible to the naked eye from low Earth orbit** under normal viewing conditions.

---

## Verdict

❌ **False**

---

## Evidence

- The wall is relatively narrow.
- It blends into the surrounding landscape.
- Visibility depends on lighting, weather conditions, and optical aids.

NASA astronauts have stated that many cities, roads, and airports are easier to identify from orbit than the Great Wall.

---

## Corrected Statement

The Great Wall of China is **generally not visible to the naked eye from space**. Under rare conditions it may be photographed using specialized equipment, but it is not uniquely visible from orbit.

---

## Final Assessment

**Confidence:** High

**Reason:** Supported by reliable scientific sources and astronaut observations.
