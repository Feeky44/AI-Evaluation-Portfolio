# Prompt Engineering #1

## Objective

Design a prompt that helps an AI generate a professional email.

---

## Prompt

You are a professional communications assistant.

Write a polite and professional email to a hiring manager thanking them for an interview.

Requirements:

- Keep the email under 180 words.
- Express appreciation for the opportunity.
- Reaffirm interest in the position.
- Maintain a warm and professional tone.
- Avoid overly casual language.
- End with an appropriate closing.

---

## Why This Prompt Works

This prompt clearly defines:

- The AI's role
- The task
- Tone
- Length
- Constraints
- Desired outcome

Providing these instructions helps the AI generate responses that are more consistent and aligned with the user's expectations.

---

## Prompt Engineering Techniques Used

- Role prompting
- Constraint prompting
- Tone specification
- Output control
- Goal-oriented prompting

---

## Expected Outcome

The AI should produce a concise, professional thank-you email that demonstrates gratitude and continued interest in the role while maintaining proper business etiquette.
